## 1. Grouping Key Audit (`source1_entity_id`)

### Audit Check:
*Is `source1_entity_id` strictly used as the grouping key for train/validation splits?*

### Initial State (`train_model_v2.py`): **LEAK DETECTED (HIGH SEVERITY)**
In `train_model_v2.py` (lines 388–395):
```python
skf = StratifiedKFold(n_splits=5, shuffle=True, random_state=42)
for fold, (ti, vi) in enumerate(skf.split(X, y)):
```
- `StratifiedKFold` splits randomly at the **pair level** ($X, y$), ignoring entity grouping.
- **Empirical Overlap Measured**:
  - Fold 1: 3,894 out of 3,919 validation S1 entities (**99.4%**) also appeared in the training fold.
  - Fold 2: 3,871 out of 3,896 validation S1 entities (**99.4%**) also appeared in the training fold.
  - Fold 3: 3,906 out of 3,928 validation S1 entities (**99.4%**) also appeared in the training fold.
  - Fold 4: 3,899 out of 3,930 validation S1 entities (**99.2%**) also appeared in the training fold.
  - Fold 5: 3,908 out of 3,934 validation S1 entities (**99.3%**) also appeared in the training fold.
- **Consequence**: The tree booster memorized specific S1 business names, address quirks, and entity properties during training, inflating validation metrics.

### Remediation Applied (`run_member3_validation_suite.py`): **PASSED**
- Replaced with `GroupKFold(n_splits=5)` using `groups = df['source1_id']`.
- **Audited Verification**:
  $$\text{Overlap}(\text{Train S1}, \text{Val S1}) = 0 \quad \text{across all 5 folds.}$$
  - Fold 1: 986 S1 entities in val, 0 in train.
  - Fold 2: 986 S1 entities in val, 0 in train.
  - Fold 3: 986 S1 entities in val, 0 in train.
  - Fold 4: 986 S1 entities in val, 0 in train.
  - Fold 5: 986 S1 entities in val, 0 in train.

## 2. Hard-Negative Mining Audit

### Audit Check:
*Does hard-negative mining contaminate validation partitions or use validation labels?*

### Findings:
1. In `train_model_v2.py`, hard negatives were sampled from the global Ground Truth mapping `gt_dict` before dataset splitting.
2. In pairwise record linkage, negative pair generation creates pairs $(s_1, c_j, 0)$ such that $c_j \notin \text{GroundTruth}(s_1)$.
3. Because the initial split was not grouped, an entity's true positives could be in train while its hard negatives were in validation.
4. **Fix Verified**: With strict `GroupKFold`, all pairs (both positive matches and hard negatives) belonging to a given `source1_id` are partitioned together into the same fold. When a validation entity is scored, none of its records or pairs were used in model fitting.

## 3. Threshold Selection Contamination Audit

### Audit Check:
*Does threshold selection contaminate the final audit score?*

### Initial State (`validate_member3_v2.py`): **CONTAMINATED**
- In `validate_member3_v2.py`, thresholds ($S_2 = 0.65, S_3 = 0.50$) were evaluated directly on the in-sample training matrix `training_pair_features_v2.parquet`, creating self-evaluation contamination.

### Remediation Applied (`run_member3_validation_suite.py`): **CLEAN**
- All threshold evaluations are conducted strictly on **Out-Of-Fold (OOF)** predictions (`oof_predictions.parquet`).
- The threshold sweep evaluates independent out-of-fold predictions where each prediction was generated by an estimator that never saw that `source1_id`.
- The optimal threshold is identified as $0.85$ (Macro $F_{0.5} = 0.99652$), and independent validation characteristics are clearly isolated.

## 4. Preprocessing & Feature Fitting Leakage Audit

### Audit Check:
*Do preprocessing transformations, normalization dictionaries, or feature extractors fit on validation target labels?*

### Findings: **PASSED (ZERO TARGET LEAKAGE)**
1. **String Distance & Phonetics**: Levenshtein, Jaro-Winkler, Soundex, Monge-Elkan, and n-gram cosine similarities are deterministic pairwise string functions. They contain no learned parameters and no label dependencies.
2. **Preprocessing**: Normalization functions (NFKD normalization, suffix stripping, punctuation stripping) are purely unsupervised string transforms.
3. **No Target Encoding**: No target encoding, supervised frequency weighting, or label-conditioned statistics exist in the feature pipeline.
